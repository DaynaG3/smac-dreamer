Limitations
-----------

- The sandbox cannot execute the real SMAClite environment, the user's JEPA
  checkpoint, CUDA workers, TorchRL memmap replay or a full learner update.
- Therefore the installer remains fail-closed: it must match and audit the
  user's exact local source before it writes anything.
- Policy improvement is not claimed. The current v1 W&B validation trend is
  negative; the hardened run must be judged by held-out macro win rate and
  original return.
