Known scientific caveat:

- The old primitive logit distribution is exactly preserved at initialization,
  but sampling a tactic consumes an RNG draw. Therefore stochastic sampled
  primitive actions are distribution-equivalent, not guaranteed bit-for-bit
  identical under an unchanged global RNG stream. Deterministic evaluation is
  identical while the residual remains zero.
